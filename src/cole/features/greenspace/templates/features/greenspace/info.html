Public parks and outdoor areas

Original add-on contribution: Margaret Worsham.

Data curation, screening and validation; COLE application functionality, logic, feature concepts, baseline Python code, development and integration: Brandon Santor.

Data: U.S. Geological Survey, Protected Areas Database of the United States (PAD-US 4.1); U.S. Census Bureau, 2021 county boundaries.

Estimated documented public outdoor area as a share of the whole county, including water. Private and nonprofit ownership, military land, and Restricted or Closed access are excluded. Unknown evidence alone contributes no area.

The inventory has varying source dates and incomplete coverage. It does not measure vegetation, travel distance or recreation quality. County details explain coverage and ownership.
