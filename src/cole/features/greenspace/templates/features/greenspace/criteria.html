Details and methods

USGS PAD-US 4.1. Estimated percentage of the whole county, including water, with public ownership and access recorded as open to the public. Parks, forests, refuges and other public outdoor areas can qualify. Private and nonprofit land, military land, and areas recorded as restricted or closed are excluded.

Enter a minimum, a maximum, or both, from 0% to 100%. Leave a field blank for no limit; entering 0 sets a zero limit. Values exactly on a limit are included. A county must meet these limits and your other filters to appear in matching results.
