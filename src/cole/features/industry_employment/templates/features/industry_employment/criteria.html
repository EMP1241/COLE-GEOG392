Details and methods

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025 Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW) annual averages. Choose industries to find counties where they rank among the top five by disclosed (publicly reported) employment, including fifth-place ties. Leave all selections blank to search without an industry filter.

All selected requires every chosen industry to qualify. Any selected requires at least one. Industry choices are independent of the number of earners.

Counts combine disclosed private and government employment covered by the source program. Withheld counts remain unknown and can change actual rankings. These are annual-average filled jobs located in the county, not job openings, jobs created, or a count of county residents.
