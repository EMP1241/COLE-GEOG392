Details and methods

FEMA National Risk Index, December 2025. COLE's classes rank modeled annual loss rates among covered counties with positive modeled loss for each hazard and measure. They are not the chance of a disaster or FEMA's community risk ratings.

Choose the highest modeled loss-rate class you will accept. Most limits use expected building loss as a share of building value; heat wave uses modeled deaths and weighted injuries per resident. Blank limits leave the search unchanged.

These are COLE-derived ranks of FEMA modeled annual loss rates, compared with covered counties having positive modeled loss for that hazard and measure. They are not disaster probabilities or FEMA's community risk ratings. Each hazard has a different comparison set.

Classes use the displayed percentile: Very Low below 20; Low from 20 to below 40; Moderate from 40 to below 60; High from 60 to below 80; Very High from 80 through 100.

Counties with missing or insufficient data for a selected hazard are excluded from matching results. Looking up a place still shows those counties and marks the missing data. “Not applicable” and “No modeled loss” are modeled statuses in this FEMA release and pass limits; they do not establish personal safety.

County averages cannot describe every address. These estimates are not forecasts or insurance ratings.
