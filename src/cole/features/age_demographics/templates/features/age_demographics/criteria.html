Details and methods

U.S. Census ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates. Explore the age distribution of all county residents. These figures describe a five-year period.

Enter a minimum, a maximum, or both. Leave a field blank for no limit; entering 0 sets a zero limit. Matching counties must meet every age limit you enter. Values exactly on a limit are included. Median age is measured in years; age-group percentages range from 0 to 100.

The age dividing residents into two equal halves. County details show its published 90% margin of error in years.

Choose age groups and set limits on their percentage of county residents. Each percentage is calculated from the group's population and the total county population. Filters use the unrounded ratio; details show one decimal place. Margins of error have not been calculated for these percentages.

Counties missing data for an age measure you selected are excluded from matching results. Looking up a place still shows its counties and marks missing values. The eight older Connecticut county boundaries used by COLE have no matching record in this ACS release.
